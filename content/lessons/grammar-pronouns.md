# Les pronoms

Le TOEIC teste surtout le **choix de la forme** selon la fonction dans la phrase.

## Tableau des formes

| Sujet | Complément | Adjectif possessif | Pronom possessif | Réfléchi |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | your | yours | yourself |
| he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | its | — | itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

## Comment choisir

1. **Avant un verbe conjugué** → pronom sujet : *She approved the plan.*
2. **Après un verbe ou une préposition** → complément : *Please send **them** the file.*
3. **Avant un nom** → adjectif possessif : *Please bring **your** badge.*
4. **Sans nom derrière** → pronom possessif : *This desk is **mine**.*
5. **Réfléchi** quand sujet et objet sont la même personne, ou pour insister : *He did it **himself**.*

## Pièges

- **its** (possessif) ≠ **it's** (it is / it has).
- **Each other / one another** = réciproque, pas un réfléchi.
- Pronom indéfini + **singulier** : *Everyone has their badge* est accepté, mais *everyone **has***.

# Pronouns

The TOEIC mostly tests **choosing the right form** for the function in the sentence.

## Table of forms

| Subject | Object | Possessive adjective | Possessive pronoun | Reflexive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | me | my | mine | myself |
| you | you | your | yours | yourself |
| he | him | his | his | himself |
| she | her | her | hers | herself |
| it | it | its | — | itself |
| we | us | our | ours | ourselves |
| they | them | their | theirs | themselves |

## How to choose

1. **Before a conjugated verb** → subject pronoun: *She approved the plan.*
2. **After a verb or preposition** → object pronoun: *Please send **them** the file.*
3. **Before a noun** → possessive adjective: *Please bring **your** badge.*
4. **With no noun after it** → possessive pronoun: *This desk is **mine**.*
5. **Reflexive** when subject and object are the same person, or for emphasis: *He did it **himself**.*

## Traps

- **its** (possessive) ≠ **it's** (it is / it has).
- **Each other / one another** = reciprocal, not reflexive.
- Indefinite pronouns take a **singular** verb: *everyone **has***.
