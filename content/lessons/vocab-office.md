# Vocabulaire : le bureau

Le quotidien du bureau revient dans toutes les parties du TOEIC : fournitures, équipements, courriers et tâches administratives.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **supplies** | fournitures | We need to order more office supplies. |
| **photocopier** | photocopieuse | The photocopier is out of paper. |
| **stationery** | papeterie | Stationery is stored in the cabinet. |
| **cubicle** | box de bureau | Her cubicle is next to the window. |
| **memo** | note de service | A memo was sent to all staff. |
| **assistant** | assistant | The assistant booked the room. |
| **receptionist** | réceptionniste | The receptionist answered the phone. |
| **file** | classer / dossier | Please file these invoices. |
| **shred** | déchiqueter | Shred confidential papers. |
| **fill out** | remplir | Please fill out this form. |
| **renew** | renouveler | Renew your badge every year. |
| **supervisor** | responsable | Ask your supervisor for approval. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **run out of** : ne plus avoir de
- **office hours** : heures d'ouverture
- **front desk** : accueil
- **hand out** : distribuer
- **sign in** : s'enregistrer

## Astuce TOEIC

Les verbes à particule (fill out, hand out, sign in) sont fréquents en Part 2 et 3 : apprenez-les en bloc.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Vocabulary: the office

Everyday office life appears in every part of the TOEIC: supplies, equipment, mail and administrative tasks.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **supplies** | items used in an office | We need to order more office supplies. |
| **photocopier** | a machine that copies documents | The photocopier is out of paper. |
| **stationery** | paper, pens and envelopes | Stationery is stored in the cabinet. |
| **cubicle** | a small work area with partitions | Her cubicle is next to the window. |
| **memo** | a short written message at work | A memo was sent to all staff. |
| **assistant** | a person who helps a manager | The assistant booked the room. |
| **receptionist** | a person who greets visitors | The receptionist answered the phone. |
| **file** | to put documents in order; a folder | Please file these invoices. |
| **shred** | to cut documents into small pieces | Shred confidential papers. |
| **fill out** | to complete a form | Please fill out this form. |
| **renew** | to extend for a new period | Renew your badge every year. |
| **supervisor** | a person who oversees work | Ask your supervisor for approval. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **run out of**
- **office hours**
- **front desk**
- **hand out**
- **sign in**

## TOEIC tip

Phrasal verbs (fill out, hand out, sign in) are frequent in Parts 2 and 3: learn them as chunks.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
