# Les verbes irréguliers à connaître

Au TOEIC, les verbes irréguliers testent surtout le **participe passé** (passif, present perfect, past perfect). Apprenez les trois formes ensemble.

## Regroupements pour retenir

- **Trois formes identiques** : *cost, cut, put, set, shut, hit, let, read* (la prononciation de *read* change).
- **Prétérit = participe passé** : *buy/bought, bring/brought, send/sent, pay/paid, meet/met, hold/held, lead/led*.
- **Voyelle qui change (i – a – u)** : *begin/began/begun, drink/drank/drunk, swim/swam/swum*.
- **Participe en -n** : *choose/chosen, drive/driven, eat/eaten, give/given, speak/spoken, take/taken, write/written*.

## Pièges des francophones

- **Ne pas régulariser** : *buyed*, *teached*, *sended* n'existent pas.
- **Ne pas confondre prétérit et participe** : *I have **wrote*** est faux → *I have **written***. *The meeting was **began*** est faux → *was **begun***.
- **Dans un passif, c'est le participe** : *The contract was **signed***, *The goods were **sold***, *The decision was **made***.

## Groups that help memory

- **Three identical forms**: *cost, cut, put, set, shut, hit, let, read* (the pronunciation of *read* changes).
- **Past = past participle**: *buy/bought, bring/brought, send/sent, pay/paid, meet/met, hold/held, lead/led*.
- **Vowel change (i – a – u)**: *begin/began/begun, drink/drank/drunk, swim/swam/swum*.
- **Participle in -n**: *choose/chosen, drive/driven, eat/eaten, give/given, speak/spoken, take/taken, write/written*.

## Traps for French speakers

- **Do not regularize**: *buyed*, *teached*, *sended* do not exist.
- **Do not mix past and participle**: "I have wrote" is wrong → *I have **written***. "The meeting was began" is wrong → *was **begun***.
- **In a passive it is the participle**: *The contract was **signed***, *The goods were **sold***, *The decision was **made***.
