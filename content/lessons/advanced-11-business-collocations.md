# Collocations professionnelles avancées

Au C1, ce n'est pas le mot isolé qui compte, mais **le mot qu'on lui associe**. Une collocation incorrecte (*do a decision*) trahit tout de suite un niveau intermédiaire.

## Les mots et expressions

| Terme | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **reach a consensus** | parvenir à un consensus | The board reached a consensus after a long debate. |
| **draw a conclusion** | tirer une conclusion | We cannot draw any conclusion from so little data. |
| **meet a requirement** | satisfaire une exigence | Applicants must meet all the requirements. |
| **raise a concern** | soulever une inquiétude | Several staff raised concerns about the schedule. |
| **shed light on** | éclairer, faire la lumière sur | The study sheds light on consumer behavior. |
| **take into account** | prendre en compte | The budget must take inflation into account. |
| **come to terms with** | accepter, se faire à | The firm has come to terms with the loss. |
| **put forward a proposal** | avancer une proposition | She put forward a proposal to cut costs. |
| **pose a threat** | représenter une menace | New competitors pose a threat to our market share. |
| **bear in mind** | garder à l'esprit | Bear in mind that prices may change. |
| **strike a balance** | trouver un équilibre | We must strike a balance between cost and quality. |
| **fall short of** | ne pas atteindre | Sales fell short of expectations. |
| **a steep decline** | une forte baisse | The quarter saw a steep decline in orders. |
| **a marked improvement** | une nette amélioration | There has been a marked improvement in morale. |
| **hold a meeting** | tenir une réunion | The committee holds a meeting every month. |
| **break the news** | annoncer (une nouvelle difficile) | He broke the news of the closure to staff. |
| **run into difficulties** | rencontrer des difficultés | The project ran into difficulties in March. |
| **launch an investigation** | ouvrir une enquête | The agency launched an investigation. |

## Comment les apprendre

Apprenez toujours **verbe + nom** ensemble. Utilisez une collocation par jour dans une phrase à vous. Au TOEIC, les collocations sont testées en Part 5 : on dit *make a decision* (jamais *do a decision*).

Ajoutez ces termes à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Advanced business collocations

At C1, what counts is not the isolated word but **the word it goes with**. A wrong collocation (*do a decision*) immediately betrays an intermediate level.

## Words and expressions

| Term | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **reach a consensus** | come to a shared agreement | The board reached a consensus after a long debate. |
| **draw a conclusion** | form a final judgment | We cannot draw any conclusion from so little data. |
| **meet a requirement** | fulfil a condition | Applicants must meet all the requirements. |
| **raise a concern** | mention a worry | Several staff raised concerns about the schedule. |
| **shed light on** | clarify | The study sheds light on consumer behavior. |
| **take into account** | consider | The budget must take inflation into account. |
| **come to terms with** | accept a difficult fact | The firm has come to terms with the loss. |
| **put forward a proposal** | suggest formally | She put forward a proposal to cut costs. |
| **pose a threat** | be a danger | New competitors pose a threat to our market share. |
| **bear in mind** | remember | Bear in mind that prices may change. |
| **strike a balance** | find a fair compromise | We must strike a balance between cost and quality. |
| **fall short of** | fail to reach | Sales fell short of expectations. |
| **a steep decline** | a sharp fall | The quarter saw a steep decline in orders. |
| **a marked improvement** | a clear improvement | There has been a marked improvement in morale. |
| **hold a meeting** | organize a meeting | The committee holds a meeting every month. |
| **break the news** | announce bad news | He broke the news of the closure to staff. |
| **run into difficulties** | face problems | The project ran into difficulties in March. |
| **launch an investigation** | start an official inquiry | The agency launched an investigation. |

## How to learn them

Always learn **verb + noun** together. Use one collocation a day in a sentence of your own. On the TOEIC, collocations are tested in Part 5: *make* a decision; *do a decision* is the error to avoid.

Add these terms to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
