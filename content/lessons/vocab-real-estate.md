# Vocabulaire : immobilier

Locations, baux et visites : l'immobilier revient en Parts 3 et 7.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **lease** | bail | The lease lasts one year. |
| **tenant** | locataire | The tenant paid the rent. |
| **landlord** | propriétaire (bailleur) | The landlord repaired the sink. |
| **rent** | loyer / louer | Rent is due on the first. |
| **property** | bien immobilier | The property is for sale. |
| **mortgage** | prêt immobilier | We took out a mortgage. |
| **vacancy** | logement disponible | No vacancies this month. |
| **utilities** | charges (eau, électricité…) | Utilities are included. |
| **renovate** | rénover | They will renovate the lobby. |
| **deposit** | dépôt de garantie | A security deposit is required. |
| **furnished** | meublé | A furnished apartment. |
| **real estate agent** | agent immobilier | The agent showed us the flat. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **sign a lease** : signer un bail
- **move in / move out** : emménager / déménager
- **spacious** : spacieux
- **within walking distance** : à distance de marche
- **square footage** : surface en pieds carrés

## Astuce TOEIC

En Part 3, les chiffres (loyer, surface, durée du bail) servent souvent de pièges : notez-les au fil de la conversation.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

*Lease* (bail), *tenant* (locataire), *landlord* (propriétaire) : le trio de base d'un dialogue immobilier.

# Vocabulary: real estate

Rentals, leases and viewings: real estate returns in Parts 3 and 7.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **lease** | a contract to rent property | The lease lasts one year. |
| **tenant** | a person who rents property | The tenant paid the rent. |
| **landlord** | a person who rents out property | The landlord repaired the sink. |
| **rent** | money paid to use property | Rent is due on the first. |
| **property** | land or buildings | The property is for sale. |
| **mortgage** | a loan to buy property | We took out a mortgage. |
| **vacancy** | an unoccupied property | No vacancies this month. |
| **utilities** | services such as gas, water and electricity | Utilities are included. |
| **renovate** | to repair and improve | They will renovate the lobby. |
| **deposit** | money held against damage | A security deposit is required. |
| **furnished** | with furniture included | A furnished apartment. |
| **real estate agent** | a person who sells or rents property | The agent showed us the flat. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **sign a lease**
- **move in / move out**
- **spacious**
- **within walking distance**
- **square footage**

## TOEIC tip

In Part 3, figures (rent, area, lease term) are often traps: note them as you listen.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
