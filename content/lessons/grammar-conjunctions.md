# Conjonctions et connecteurs

Trois familles à bien distinguer : **conjonctions de coordination**, **conjonctions de subordination** et **adverbes de liaison**.

## Les trois familles

| Famille | Exemples | Place / ponctuation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coordination | and, but, or, so, yet | relie deux éléments ; virgule avant si deux propositions |
| Subordination | although, because, while, if, unless, since, once, whereas | introduit une proposition **avec sujet + verbe** |
| Adverbes de liaison | however, therefore, moreover, in addition, otherwise | en tête de phrase ou après un **point-virgule**, suivis d'une virgule |

## Sens à retenir

- **Cause** : because, since, as → *because of / due to* + **nom**
- **Opposition** : although, though, even though, whereas, while → *despite / in spite of* + **nom**
- **Condition** : if, unless, provided that, as long as
- **But** : so that, in order to
- **Temps** : when, while, as soon as, once, until, before, after

## Pièges

1. **Conjonction + nom** est faux : *Although **the rain*** → *Although it rained* ou *Despite the rain*.
2. **Adverbe de liaison ne relie pas** deux phrases sans ponctuation : *…; however, …*.
3. **Unless** a déjà un sens négatif : pas de *not* derrière.

# Conjunctions and connectors

Three families to tell apart: **coordinating conjunctions**, **subordinating conjunctions** and **conjunctive adverbs**.

## The three families

| Family | Examples | Position / punctuation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coordinating | and, but, or, so, yet | joins two elements; comma before if joining two clauses |
| Subordinating | although, because, while, if, unless, since, once, whereas | introduces a clause **with subject + verb** |
| Conjunctive adverbs | however, therefore, moreover, in addition, otherwise | sentence start or after a **semicolon**, followed by a comma |

## Meanings to remember

- **Cause**: because, since, as → *because of / due to* + **noun**
- **Contrast**: although, though, even though, whereas, while → *despite / in spite of* + **noun**
- **Condition**: if, unless, provided that, as long as
- **Purpose**: so that, in order to
- **Time**: when, while, as soon as, once, until, before, after

## Traps

1. **Conjunction + noun** is wrong: *Although **the rain*** → *Although it rained* or *Despite the rain*.
2. **A conjunctive adverb does not join** two sentences without punctuation: *…; however, …*.
3. **Unless** already means "if not": no extra *not* after it.
