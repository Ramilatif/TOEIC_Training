# Vocabulary: health

Medical appointments, prescriptions, insurance: health topics appear mostly in Parts 2, 3 and 7.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **appointment** | a planned meeting with a professional | I have a dentist appointment. |
| **prescription** | a doctor's written order for medicine | Pick up your prescription. |
| **pharmacy** | a place that sells medicine | The pharmacy closes at eight. |
| **symptom** | a sign of illness | Fever is a common symptom. |
| **insurance** | protection against costs | Health insurance covers this. |
| **checkup** | a routine medical examination | Schedule an annual checkup. |
| **patient** | a person receiving care | The patient waited an hour. |
| **clinic** | a small medical center | The clinic opens at seven. |
| **treatment** | medical care for an illness | The treatment is effective. |
| **recover** | to become healthy again | She is recovering well. |
| **allergy** | a bad reaction to a substance | He has a nut allergy. |
| **vaccination** | an injection that protects | Vaccination is free. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **make an appointment**
- **be covered by**
- **take a day off sick**
- **side effect**
- **get well soon**

## TOEIC tip

In Part 2, a question about an appointment often gets an answer about time or availability, not just 'yes'.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
