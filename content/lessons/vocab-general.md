# Vocabulaire : affaires en général

Les mots transversaux des affaires : contrats, partenariats, objectifs. Ils apparaissent dans tous les types de textes.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **contract** | contrat | Sign the contract. |
| **merger** | fusion | The merger was announced. |
| **acquire** | acquérir | The firm acquired a rival. |
| **headquarters** | siège social | Headquarters is in Paris. |
| **subsidiary** | filiale | The subsidiary opened in Spain. |
| **deadline** | date limite | Meet the deadline. |
| **negotiate** | négocier | We negotiated the terms. |
| **client** | client | The client approved. |
| **proposal** | proposition | Submit your proposal. |
| **regarding** | concernant | Regarding your request, yes. |
| **approximately** | environ | Approximately 200 attended. |
| **on behalf of** | au nom de | I speak on behalf of the team. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **make a profit** : réaliser un bénéfice
- **reach an agreement** : parvenir à un accord
- **take over** : reprendre
- **set a goal** : fixer un objectif
- **in charge of** : responsable de

## Astuce TOEIC

« Regarding », « concerning » et « with respect to » ouvrent presque tous les emails de Part 7 : repérez-les pour trouver l'objet.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Vocabulary: general business

Cross-cutting business words: contracts, partnerships, goals. They appear in every kind of text.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **contract** | a legal agreement | Sign the contract. |
| **merger** | the joining of two companies | The merger was announced. |
| **acquire** | to obtain or buy | The firm acquired a rival. |
| **headquarters** | the main office | Headquarters is in Paris. |
| **subsidiary** | a company owned by another | The subsidiary opened in Spain. |
| **deadline** | the latest time to finish | Meet the deadline. |
| **negotiate** | to discuss to reach an agreement | We negotiated the terms. |
| **client** | a person who pays for services | The client approved. |
| **proposal** | a formal suggestion | Submit your proposal. |
| **regarding** | about | Regarding your request, yes. |
| **approximately** | about, roughly | Approximately 200 attended. |
| **on behalf of** | representing someone | I speak on behalf of the team. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **make a profit**
- **reach an agreement**
- **take over**
- **set a goal**
- **in charge of**

## TOEIC tip

'Regarding', 'concerning' and 'with respect to' open almost every Part 7 email: spot them to find the subject.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
