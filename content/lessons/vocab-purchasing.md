# Vocabulaire : achats et ventes

Commandes, remises, livraisons : un thème omniprésent dans les courriels et annonces du TOEIC.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **order** | commande / commander | We placed an order on Monday. |
| **receipt** | reçu | Keep your receipt. |
| **refund** | remboursement | You may request a refund. |
| **warranty** | garantie | The warranty lasts two years. |
| **discount** | remise | We offer a 10 percent discount. |
| **shipment** | expédition | The shipment arrived late. |
| **delivery** | livraison | Free delivery is included. |
| **out of stock** | en rupture de stock | That model is out of stock. |
| **purchase** | achat / acheter | Your purchase is confirmed. |
| **coupon** | bon de réduction | Use this coupon at checkout. |
| **retailer** | détaillant | The retailer opened a new store. |
| **wholesale** | en gros | Wholesale prices apply. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **place an order** : passer commande
- **in stock** : en stock
- **free of charge** : gratuit
- **back order** : commande en attente
- **terms and conditions** : conditions générales

## Astuce TOEIC

Les textes de Part 7 sur les commandes utilisent souvent « back order » et « out of stock » : repérez ce qui retarde la livraison.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

<!-- lang:en -->
# Vocabulary: purchasing and sales

Orders, discounts, deliveries: a theme found throughout TOEIC emails and ads.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **order** | a request to buy goods | We placed an order on Monday. |
| **receipt** | proof of payment | Keep your receipt. |
| **refund** | money given back | You may request a refund. |
| **warranty** | a promise to repair or replace | The warranty lasts two years. |
| **discount** | a reduction in price | We offer a 10 percent discount. |
| **shipment** | goods sent together | The shipment arrived late. |
| **delivery** | bringing goods to a place | Free delivery is included. |
| **out of stock** | not available at present | That model is out of stock. |
| **purchase** | to buy; something bought | Your purchase is confirmed. |
| **coupon** | a ticket giving a discount | Use this coupon at checkout. |
| **retailer** | a business selling to the public | The retailer opened a new store. |
| **wholesale** | selling in large amounts | Wholesale prices apply. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **place an order**
- **in stock**
- **free of charge**
- **back order**
- **terms and conditions**

## TOEIC tip

Part 7 texts about orders often use 'back order' and 'out of stock': look for what delays delivery.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
