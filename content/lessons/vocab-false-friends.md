# Faux amis français-anglais

Certains mots anglais ressemblent à des mots français mais ont un autre sens. Le TOEIC en profite : ces pièges coûtent des points aux francophones.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **actually** | en fait (pas « actuellement ») | Actually, the meeting is on Monday. |
| **currently** | actuellement | She is currently abroad. |
| **eventually** | finalement (pas « éventuellement ») | He eventually accepted the offer. |
| **possibly** | peut-être | We could possibly finish by noon. |
| **sensible** | raisonnable (pas « sensible ») | It is a sensible decision. |
| **sensitive** | sensible, délicat | This is sensitive information. |
| **assist** | aider (pas « assister à ») | Can I assist you? |
| **attend** | assister à, participer | Over 200 people attended. |
| **advertisement** | publicité, annonce | I saw your advertisement. |
| **deceive** | tromper (pas « décevoir ») | Do not deceive customers. |
| **disappoint** | décevoir | The results disappointed investors. |
| **introduce** | présenter (une personne) | Let me introduce my colleague. |
| **rest** | reste, repos | The rest of the staff is here. |
| **library** | bibliothèque (pas « librairie ») | The library closes at six. |
| **lecture** | cours magistral (pas « lecture ») | The lecture lasts an hour. |
| **formation** | constitution (pas « formation professionnelle ») | The formation of the committee took weeks. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **attend a meeting** : assister à une réunion
- **take part in** : participer à
- **make an effort** : faire un effort
- **training (not formation)** : formation professionnelle
- **a sensible plan** : un plan raisonnable
- **in fact / actually** : en fait

## Astuce TOEIC

Quand vous hésitez devant un mot qui ressemble au français, demandez-vous : « le sens du contexte colle-t-il ? ». *Actually*, *currently*, *eventually* sont les trois pièges les plus fréquents.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

<!-- lang:en -->
# False friends (French vs English)

Some English words look like French words but mean something else. The TOEIC uses them: they cost French speakers points.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **actually** | in fact | Actually, the meeting is on Monday. |
| **currently** | at present | She is currently abroad. |
| **eventually** | in the end | He eventually accepted the offer. |
| **possibly** | perhaps | We could possibly finish by noon. |
| **sensible** | reasonable | It is a sensible decision. |
| **sensitive** | easily affected | This is sensitive information. |
| **assist** | help | Can I assist you? |
| **attend** | be present at | Over 200 people attended. |
| **advertisement** | a promotion | I saw your advertisement. |
| **deceive** | mislead | Do not deceive customers. |
| **disappoint** | let down | The results disappointed investors. |
| **introduce** | present someone | Let me introduce my colleague. |
| **rest** | remainder | The rest of the staff is here. |
| **library** | a place with books to borrow | The library closes at six. |
| **lecture** | a talk | The lecture lasts an hour. |
| **formation** | the act of forming | The formation of the committee took weeks. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **attend a meeting**
- **take part in**
- **make an effort**
- **training (not formation)**
- **a sensible plan**
- **in fact / actually**

## TOEIC tip

When a word looks like French, ask yourself whether the context really fits. *Actually*, *currently*, *eventually* are the three most frequent traps.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
