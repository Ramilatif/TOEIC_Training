# Vocabulaire formel et académique

Le registre formel remplace les mots courants par des équivalents plus précis. Les connaître permet de comprendre les textes longs (Part 7) et de **reformuler** : le TOEIC aime proposer le synonyme formel d'un mot du texte.

## Les mots et expressions

| Terme | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **mitigate** | atténuer | Measures to mitigate the risk were adopted. |
| **exacerbate** | aggraver | The delay exacerbated the shortage. |
| **facilitate** | faciliter | The new tool facilitates collaboration. |
| **undertake** | entreprendre | The firm will undertake a full review. |
| **endeavor** | s'efforcer | We endeavor to reply within two days. |
| **ascertain** | établir, s'assurer de | Please ascertain the cause of the failure. |
| **commence** | commencer | Construction will commence in May. |
| **subsequent** | ultérieur | Subsequent meetings will be held online. |
| **prior to** | avant | Please sign prior to departure. |
| **pursuant to** | conformément à | Pursuant to the contract, payment is due in 30 days. |
| **inadvertently** | par inadvertance | The file was inadvertently deleted. |
| **substantial** | considérable | The firm made a substantial profit. |
| **negligible** | négligeable | The impact on costs is negligible. |
| **preliminary** | préliminaire | A preliminary report is available. |
| **comprehensive** | complet, exhaustif | We offer a comprehensive range of services. |
| **viable** | viable, réalisable | This is a viable alternative. |
| **ambiguous** | ambigu | The clause is ambiguous. |
| **inherent** | inhérent | Risk is inherent in any investment. |
| **rectify** | rectifier | We will rectify the error at once. |
| **reinforce** | renforcer | The data reinforce our conclusion. |

## Comment les apprendre

Associez chaque mot formel à son **synonyme courant** : *commence = begin*, *prior to = before*, *ascertain = find out*, *facilitate = make easier*. Au TOEIC, on vous donne l'un et on attend l'autre.

Ajoutez ces termes à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Formal and academic vocabulary

The formal register replaces everyday words with more precise equivalents. Knowing them helps with long texts (Part 7) and with **paraphrase**: the TOEIC likes to offer the formal synonym of a word from the text.

## Words and expressions

| Term | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **mitigate** | make less severe | Measures to mitigate the risk were adopted. |
| **exacerbate** | make worse | The delay exacerbated the shortage. |
| **facilitate** | make easier | The new tool facilitates collaboration. |
| **undertake** | begin and carry out | The firm will undertake a full review. |
| **endeavor** | try hard | We endeavor to reply within two days. |
| **ascertain** | find out for certain | Please ascertain the cause of the failure. |
| **commence** | begin | Construction will commence in May. |
| **subsequent** | coming after | Subsequent meetings will be held online. |
| **prior to** | before | Please sign prior to departure. |
| **pursuant to** | in accordance with | Pursuant to the contract, payment is due in 30 days. |
| **inadvertently** | by mistake | The file was inadvertently deleted. |
| **substantial** | large in amount | The firm made a substantial profit. |
| **negligible** | so small it can be ignored | The impact on costs is negligible. |
| **preliminary** | coming before the main stage | A preliminary report is available. |
| **comprehensive** | covering everything | We offer a comprehensive range of services. |
| **viable** | able to work or succeed | This is a viable alternative. |
| **ambiguous** | open to more than one meaning | The clause is ambiguous. |
| **inherent** | forming a natural part | Risk is inherent in any investment. |
| **rectify** | put right | We will rectify the error at once. |
| **reinforce** | strengthen | The data reinforce our conclusion. |

## How to learn them

Pair each formal word with its **everyday synonym**: *commence = begin*, *prior to = before*, *ascertain = find out*, *facilitate = make easier*. On the TOEIC you are given one and expected to find the other.

Add these terms to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
