# Gérondif et infinitif

Après un verbe, on choisit entre **-ing** (gérondif) et **to + verbe** (infinitif) selon le verbe principal.

## Verbes suivis du gérondif (-ing)

*enjoy, avoid, consider, suggest, recommend, postpone, delay, finish, deny, mind, keep, risk, discuss, admit.*
*The firm **postponed launching** the product.*

## Verbes suivis de l'infinitif (to)

*decide, plan, expect, hope, agree, refuse, offer, promise, manage, fail, afford, need, want, intend.*
*We **decided to hire** two analysts.*

## Les deux sens possibles

*begin, start, continue, like, prefer* : peu de différence de sens.
*stop, remember, forget* : **sens différent** (*stop doing* = arrêter de ; *stop to do* = s'arrêter pour).

## « To » préposition → gérondif

*look forward **to**, be committed **to**, be used **to**, object **to**, contribute **to**, in addition **to*** → **-ing**.
*We look forward to **hearing** from you.*

## Autres usages

- **Après une préposition** : toujours le gérondif : *by **signing***, *before **leaving***.
- **But** : *in order **to** + verbe*.
- **Adjectif + to + verbe** : *ready to start*, *eager to learn*.

# Gerund and infinitive

After a verb, choose between **-ing** (gerund) and **to + verb** (infinitive) depending on the main verb.

## Verbs followed by the gerund (-ing)

*enjoy, avoid, consider, suggest, recommend, postpone, delay, finish, deny, mind, keep, risk, discuss, admit.*
*The firm **postponed launching** the product.*

## Verbs followed by the infinitive (to)

*decide, plan, expect, hope, agree, refuse, offer, promise, manage, fail, afford, need, want, intend.*
*We **decided to hire** two analysts.*

## Both possible

*begin, start, continue, like, prefer*: little difference in meaning.
*stop, remember, forget*: **different meaning** (*stop doing* = quit; *stop to do* = pause in order to).

## "To" as a preposition → gerund

*look forward **to**, be committed **to**, be used **to**, object **to**, contribute **to**, in addition **to*** → **-ing**.
*We look forward to **hearing** from you.*

## Other uses

- **After a preposition**: always the gerund: *by **signing***, *before **leaving***.
- **Purpose**: *in order **to** + verb*.
- **Adjective + to + verb**: *ready to start*, *eager to learn*.
