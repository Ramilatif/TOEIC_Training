# L'accord sujet-verbe

Le verbe s'accorde avec le **noyau du sujet**, pas avec le mot qui le précède ou le suit.

## Règles essentielles

| Cas | Règle | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each, every, either, neither, one | verbe au **singulier** | **Each** of the managers **has** an office. |
| Both, several, few, many | verbe au **pluriel** | **Several** employees **were** late. |
| A number of + pluriel | pluriel | **A number of** staff **are** absent. |
| The number of + pluriel | singulier | **The number of** staff **is** growing. |
| Sujets liés par *and* | pluriel | The manager **and** the clerk **are** here. |
| Either…or / neither…nor | s'accorde avec le sujet **le plus proche** | Neither the staff nor the **manager is** ready. |
| Nom collectif (committee, team) | singulier (usage américain) | The team **is** meeting now. |

## Pièges

1. **Mot intercalé** : *The list of items **is** long.* Le sujet est *list*, pas *items*.
2. **Sujet inversé** : *There **are** three files on the desk.* Le vrai sujet suit le verbe.
3. **Noms toujours singuliers** : *news, information, advice, equipment* → *The information **is** useful.*

## Astuce TOEIC

Barrez mentalement tout ce qui est entre le sujet et le verbe (groupes en *of*, *with*, *as well as*, virgules) pour retrouver le noyau.

<!-- extra -->
## Pièges des francophones

- **Le noyau du sujet, pas le mot le plus proche** : *The list of new products **is** ready.* (sujet = *list*).
- **Each, every, either, neither + singulier** : *Each of the employees **has** a badge.*
- **Sujets collectifs** (*team, staff, committee*) : verbe au singulier en anglais américain (*The staff **is** meeting*).
- **Mots toujours pluriels pour un français ≠ anglais** : *news, information, advice, equipment, furniture* sont **non dénombrables** et singuliers : *The news **is** good.* (jamais *informations*, *advices*).
- **A number of + pluriel / The number of + singulier** : *A number of clients **are** waiting* / *The number of clients **is** growing*.

## Exemples en contexte

- *Neither the manager nor the assistants **were** informed.* (le verbe s'accorde avec le sujet le plus proche)
- *The information in these files **is** confidential.*

# Subject-verb agreement

The verb agrees with the **head of the subject**, not with the word right before or after it.

## Key rules

| Case | Rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each, every, either, neither, one | **singular** verb | **Each** of the managers **has** an office. |
| Both, several, few, many | **plural** verb | **Several** employees **were** late. |
| A number of + plural | plural | **A number of** staff **are** absent. |
| The number of + plural | singular | **The number of** staff **is** growing. |
| Subjects joined by *and* | plural | The manager **and** the clerk **are** here. |
| Either…or / neither…nor | agrees with the **nearest** subject | Neither the staff nor the **manager is** ready. |
| Collective noun (committee, team) | singular (American usage) | The team **is** meeting now. |

## Traps

1. **Interrupting words**: *The list of items **is** long.* The subject is *list*, not *items*.
2. **Inverted subject**: *There **are** three files on the desk.* The real subject follows the verb.
3. **Always-singular nouns**: *news, information, advice, equipment* → *The information **is** useful.*

## TOEIC tip

Mentally cross out everything between subject and verb (*of*-groups, *with*, *as well as*, commas) to find the head noun.

## Traps for French speakers

- **The head of the subject, not the nearest word**: *The list of new products **is** ready.* (subject = *list*).
- **Each, every, either, neither + singular**: *Each of the employees **has** a badge.*
- **Collective subjects** (*team, staff, committee*): singular verb in American English (*The staff **is** meeting*).
- **Uncountable nouns**: *news, information, advice, equipment, furniture* are singular: *The news **is** good.* (never "informations", "advices").
- **A number of + plural / The number of + singular**: *A number of clients **are** waiting* / *The number of clients **is** growing*.

## Examples in context

- *Neither the manager nor the assistants **were** informed.* (verb agrees with the nearer subject)
- *The information in these files **is** confidential.*
