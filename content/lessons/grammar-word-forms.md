# Familles de mots

La question la plus fréquente de la Part 5 : **quelle catégorie grammaticale** attend le blanc ?

## Terminaisons typiques

| Catégorie | Terminaisons | Exemples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nom** | -tion, -sion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -ance/-ence, -er/-or (personne), -ship | decision, payment, quality, manager |
| **Verbe** | -ize, -ify, -en, -ate | organize, simplify, widen, create |
| **Adjectif** | -ful, -less, -ous, -ive, -able/-ible, -al, -ic, -ent/-ant | useful, effective, reliable |
| **Adverbe** | -ly | quickly, effectively |

## Où se placent-ils ?

1. **Après un article / un possessif / un adjectif** → **nom** : *the **decision***, *our new **policy***.
2. **Avant un nom** → **adjectif** : *an **effective** plan*.
3. **Avant ou après un verbe, ou devant un adjectif** → **adverbe** : *works **efficiently***, *highly **qualified***.
4. **Après un sujet, ou un modal / to** → **verbe** : *to **organize***.
5. **Après *be* ou *become*** → **adjectif** (ou nom) : *The plan is **effective***.

## Piège

Gardez le sens : *economic* (qui concerne l'économie) ≠ *economical* (économe) ; *considerable* (important) ≠ *considerate* (prévenant).

<!-- extra -->
## Pièges des francophones

- **Raisonner par position** : *après un article/adjectif → nom* ; *après un sujet → verbe* ; *avant un adjectif → adverbe* ; *entre « be » et un nom → adjectif*.
- **Suffixes de noms** : -tion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -ance/-ence, -er/-or (personne). **Adjectifs** : -ful, -ous, -able, -ive, -al, -ic. **Adverbes** : -ly.
- **Faux amis de dérivation** : *economic* (relatif à l'économie) ≠ *economical* (économique, bon marché) ; *considerable* (important) ≠ *considerate* (prévenant).
- **Adverbes sans -ly** : *fast, hard, late* (≠ *hardly*, *lately*).

## Exemples en contexte

- *The **successful** applicant will start in May.*
- *Prices have risen **significantly**.*

# Word forms

The most frequent Part 5 question: **which part of speech** does the blank need?

## Typical endings

| Category | Endings | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Noun** | -tion, -sion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -ance/-ence, -er/-or (person), -ship | decision, payment, quality, manager |
| **Verb** | -ize, -ify, -en, -ate | organize, simplify, widen, create |
| **Adjective** | -ful, -less, -ous, -ive, -able/-ible, -al, -ic, -ent/-ant | useful, effective, reliable |
| **Adverb** | -ly | quickly, effectively |

## Where do they go?

1. **After an article / possessive / adjective** → **noun**: *the **decision***, *our new **policy***.
2. **Before a noun** → **adjective**: *an **effective** plan*.
3. **Before or after a verb, or before an adjective** → **adverb**: *works **efficiently***, *highly **qualified***.
4. **After a subject, a modal or "to"** → **verb**: *to **organize***.
5. **After *be* or *become*** → **adjective** (or noun): *The plan is **effective***.

## Trap

Keep the meaning in mind: *economic* (about the economy) ≠ *economical* (thrifty); *considerable* (large) ≠ *considerate* (thoughtful).

## Traps for French speakers

- **Reason by position**: *after an article/adjective → noun*; *after a subject → verb*; *before an adjective → adverb*; *between "be" and a noun → adjective*.
- **Noun suffixes**: -tion, -ment, -ness, -ity, -ance/-ence, -er/-or (person). **Adjectives**: -ful, -ous, -able, -ive, -al, -ic. **Adverbs**: -ly.
- **Derivation look-alikes**: *economic* (about the economy) ≠ *economical* (cheap, thrifty); *considerable* (large) ≠ *considerate* (thoughtful).
- **Adverbs without -ly**: *fast, hard, late* (≠ *hardly*, *lately*).

## Examples in context

- *The **successful** applicant will start in May.*
- *Prices have risen **significantly**.*
