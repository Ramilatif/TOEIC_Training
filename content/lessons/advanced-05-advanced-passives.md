# Passifs avancés et construction causative

## 1. Passif impersonnel avec verbes de communication ou d'opinion

Pour exprimer une information **sans en citer la source** (le « on » français), on utilise deux structures :

| Structure | Exemple |
| --- | --- |
| **It + be + participe + that + proposition** | *It **is said that** the firm is profitable.* |
| **Sujet + be + participe + to + infinitif** | *The firm **is said to be** profitable.* |

Verbes courants : *say, believe, think, report, expect, know, consider, understand, estimate, announce*.

- Même temps : *He is believed **to be** abroad.* (présent)
- Antériorité : *He is believed **to have left**.* (action antérieure à la croyance)
- Progressif : *The market is expected **to be recovering** by June.*

## 2. La construction causative

| Forme | Sens | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **have + objet + participe passé** | faire faire (service) | *We **had** the contract **reviewed** by a lawyer.* |
| **get + objet + participe passé** | idem, plus familier | *I must **get** my laptop **repaired**.* |
| **have + personne + base** | faire faire à quelqu'un | *She **had** the assistant **book** the flights.* |
| **get + personne + to + infinitif** | convaincre quelqu'un de | *I **got** him **to sign**.* |
| **make + personne + base** | contraindre | *The delay **made** us **change** our plans.* |
| **let + personne + base** | permettre | *They **let** staff **work** from home.* |

## 3. Passifs à deux compléments

- *We were **offered** a discount.* (la personne devient sujet)
- *A discount was offered **to us**.*
- Verbes : *give, offer, send, show, tell, promise*.

## 4. Passif des verbes à particule

*The proposal was **turned down**.* · *The meeting was **called off**.* · *The error was **pointed out**.* (la particule reste collée au verbe)

## 5. Passif avec *being* (gérondif passif) et infinitif passif

- *She dislikes **being interrupted**.*
- *The report needs **to be updated**.*
- *He wants **to have been informed** earlier.* (infinitif parfait passif)

## Pièges des francophones

- **have something done ≠ do it yourself** : *I **had** my car repaired* = quelqu'un l'a réparée.
- **Pas de passif avec les verbes intransitifs** : *occur, happen, remain, seem* (sauf *It is said*).
- **Participe passé obligatoire** : *had the report **written*** (pas *wrote*).

<!-- lang:en -->
# Advanced passives and causatives

## 1. Impersonal passive with reporting or opinion verbs

To present information **without naming the source** (the French "on"), two structures are used:

| Structure | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **It + be + participle + that + clause** | *It **is said that** the firm is profitable.* |
| **Subject + be + participle + to + infinitive** | *The firm **is said to be** profitable.* |

Common verbs: *say, believe, think, report, expect, know, consider, understand, estimate, announce*.

- Same time: *He is believed **to be** abroad.* (present)
- Earlier action: *He is believed **to have left**.* (before the belief)
- Progressive: *The market is expected **to be recovering** by June.*

## 2. The causative construction

| Form | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **have + object + past participle** | have something done (service) | *We **had** the contract **reviewed** by a lawyer.* |
| **get + object + past participle** | same, more informal | *I must **get** my laptop **repaired**.* |
| **have + person + base** | have someone do | *She **had** the assistant **book** the flights.* |
| **get + person + to + infinitive** | persuade someone to | *I **got** him **to sign**.* |
| **make + person + base** | force | *The delay **made** us **change** our plans.* |
| **let + person + base** | allow | *They **let** staff **work** from home.* |

## 3. Passives with two objects

- *We were **offered** a discount.* (the person becomes the subject)
- *A discount was offered **to us**.*
- Verbs: *give, offer, send, show, tell, promise*.

## 4. Passive of phrasal verbs

*The proposal was **turned down**.* · *The meeting was **called off**.* · *The error was **pointed out**.* (the particle stays with the verb)

## 5. Passive with *being* (passive gerund) and passive infinitive

- *She dislikes **being interrupted**.*
- *The report needs **to be updated**.*
- *He wants **to have been informed** earlier.* (perfect passive infinitive)

## Traps for French speakers

- **have something done ≠ do it yourself**: *I **had** my car repaired* = someone repaired it.
- **No passive with intransitive verbs**: *occur, happen, remain, seem* (except *It is said*).
- **Past participle required**: *had the report **written*** (not *wrote*).
