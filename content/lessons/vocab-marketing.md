# Vocabulaire : marketing

Campagnes, clientèle, marques : le marketing nourrit de nombreuses annonces et articles de Part 7.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **campaign** | campagne | The campaign starts in May. |
| **target audience** | public cible | Our target audience is young adults. |
| **brand** | marque | The brand is well known. |
| **launch** | lancer / lancement | We launch the product in June. |
| **survey** | enquête | Complete our customer survey. |
| **advertisement** | publicité | An advertisement ran in the paper. |
| **slogan** | slogan | Their slogan is easy to remember. |
| **market share** | part de marché | Our market share grew. |
| **promote** | promouvoir | We promote our new line online. |
| **loyalty program** | programme de fidélité | Join our loyalty program. |
| **sponsor** | parrainer | The firm sponsors the marathon. |
| **trend** | tendance | A growing trend in e-commerce. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **launch a campaign** : lancer une campagne
- **conduct a survey** : mener une enquête
- **raise awareness** : sensibiliser
- **word of mouth** : bouche-à-oreille
- **limited time offer** : offre à durée limitée

## Astuce TOEIC

Dans une publicité de Part 4, la date limite et l'avantage offert sont presque toujours demandés : écoutez « until », « only », « free ».

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Vocabulary: marketing

Campaigns, customers, brands: marketing feeds many ads and articles in Part 7.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **campaign** | a planned set of promotional activities | The campaign starts in May. |
| **target audience** | the group a product is aimed at | Our target audience is young adults. |
| **brand** | a product's name and image | The brand is well known. |
| **launch** | to introduce a new product | We launch the product in June. |
| **survey** | a set of questions asked to many people | Complete our customer survey. |
| **advertisement** | a public notice promoting something | An advertisement ran in the paper. |
| **slogan** | a short catchy phrase | Their slogan is easy to remember. |
| **market share** | a company's portion of sales | Our market share grew. |
| **promote** | to advertise or support | We promote our new line online. |
| **loyalty program** | rewards for repeat customers | Join our loyalty program. |
| **sponsor** | to support financially | The firm sponsors the marathon. |
| **trend** | a general direction of change | A growing trend in e-commerce. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **launch a campaign**
- **conduct a survey**
- **raise awareness**
- **word of mouth**
- **limited time offer**

## TOEIC tip

In a Part 4 advertisement, the deadline and the benefit offered are almost always asked: listen for 'until', 'only', 'free'.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
