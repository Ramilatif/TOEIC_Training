# Verbes à particule avancés et verbes prépositionnels

Les verbes à particule de niveau C1 ont souvent un **sens figuré** et un **synonyme formel**. Les connaître double votre capacité à comprendre les dialogues rapides.

## Les mots et expressions

| Terme | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **come up against** | se heurter à | We came up against strong opposition. |
| **bring about** | provoquer, entraîner | The new law brought about major changes. |
| **carry over** | reporter (sur la période suivante) | Unused days carry over to next year. |
| **phase out** | supprimer progressivement | The firm will phase out paper invoices. |
| **roll out** | déployer | The update will be rolled out in May. |
| **scale back** | réduire, diminuer | The company scaled back its expansion plans. |
| **step down** | démissionner (d'un poste) | The CEO will step down in June. |
| **take on** | embaucher / prendre en charge | We are taking on ten new staff. |
| **lay off** | licencier (pour raisons économiques) | The firm laid off 200 workers. |
| **draw up** | rédiger, établir | Please draw up a draft contract. |
| **iron out** | régler (un problème) | We need to iron out a few details. |
| **boil down to** | se résumer à | It boils down to a question of price. |
| **fall through** | échouer, tomber à l'eau | The deal fell through at the last minute. |
| **pick up** | reprendre, s'améliorer | Business picked up in March. |
| **hold off** | retarder, patienter | We will hold off on hiring until June. |
| **sign off on** | valider, approuver | The director signed off on the budget. |
| **fall behind** | prendre du retard | We have fallen behind schedule. |
| **look into** | examiner, enquêter sur | We will look into the complaint. |

## Comment les apprendre

Regroupez les verbes par **thème** (changement : *phase out, scale back, roll out* ; personnel : *take on, lay off, step down*) et apprenez le synonyme formel : *bring about = cause*, *draw up = prepare*, *sign off on = approve*.

Ajoutez ces termes à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Advanced phrasal and prepositional verbs

C1-level phrasal verbs often have a **figurative meaning** and a **formal synonym**. Knowing them doubles your ability to follow fast dialogues.

## Words and expressions

| Term | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **come up against** | meet a difficulty | We came up against strong opposition. |
| **bring about** | cause to happen | The new law brought about major changes. |
| **carry over** | move to the next period | Unused days carry over to next year. |
| **phase out** | stop gradually | The firm will phase out paper invoices. |
| **roll out** | launch progressively | The update will be rolled out in May. |
| **scale back** | reduce in size | The company scaled back its expansion plans. |
| **step down** | resign from a position | The CEO will step down in June. |
| **take on** | hire / accept a task | We are taking on ten new staff. |
| **lay off** | dismiss for economic reasons | The firm laid off 200 workers. |
| **draw up** | prepare in writing | Please draw up a draft contract. |
| **iron out** | resolve small problems | We need to iron out a few details. |
| **boil down to** | come down to | It boils down to a question of price. |
| **fall through** | fail to happen | The deal fell through at the last minute. |
| **pick up** | improve or resume | Business picked up in March. |
| **hold off** | delay | We will hold off on hiring until June. |
| **sign off on** | approve formally | The director signed off on the budget. |
| **fall behind** | fail to keep up | We have fallen behind schedule. |
| **look into** | investigate | We will look into the complaint. |

## How to learn them

Group verbs by **theme** (change: *phase out, scale back, roll out*; staff: *take on, lay off, step down*) and learn the formal synonym: *bring about = cause*, *draw up = prepare*, *sign off on = approve*.

Add these terms to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
