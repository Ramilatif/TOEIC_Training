# Le présent : simple et continu

## Présent simple

| Personne | Affirmatif | Négatif | Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I / you / we / they | work | do not (don't) work | Do you work? |
| he / she / it | work**s** | does not (doesn't) work | Does he work? |

Le **-s** de la 3e personne est la seule marque de conjugaison du présent.

**Emplois** : habitudes (*She **arrives** at 8.*), vérités générales, horaires (*The train **leaves** at 6.*).

**Indices** : *always, usually, often, every day, never, on Mondays*.

## Présent continu : am / is / are + -ing

| Personne | Affirmatif | Négatif | Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | am working | am not working | Am I working? |
| he / she / it | is working | is not working | Is she working? |
| we / you / they | are working | are not working | Are they working? |

**Emplois** : action en cours (*We **are reviewing** the contract.*), situation temporaire, projet arrangé (*I **am meeting** the client tomorrow.*).

**Indices** : *now, at the moment, currently, this week*.

## Orthographe du -s

| Fin du verbe | Règle | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| consonne + **y** | y → **ies** | study → studies |
| **s, sh, ch, x, z, o** | + **es** | watch → watches, go → goes |
| le reste | + **s** | work → works |

## Pièges des francophones

- **« Je travaille ici depuis 2 ans »** ≠ présent : *I have worked here for two years.*
- **Verbes d'état** (*know, need, want, like, belong, own, understand*) : jamais au continu. *I **know** him* (pas *I am knowing*).
- **Le -s ne se met qu'à la 3e personne du singulier** : *The managers **work*** (pluriel, pas de -s), *The manager **works***.

# The present: simple and continuous

## Simple present

| Person | Affirmative | Negative | Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I / you / we / they | work | do not (don't) work | Do you work? |
| he / she / it | work**s** | does not (doesn't) work | Does he work? |

The **-s** of the third person is the only conjugation mark in the present.

**Uses**: habits (*She **arrives** at 8.*), general truths, timetables (*The train **leaves** at 6.*).

**Clues**: *always, usually, often, every day, never, on Mondays*.

## Present continuous: am / is / are + -ing

| Person | Affirmative | Negative | Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | am working | am not working | Am I working? |
| he / she / it | is working | is not working | Is she working? |
| we / you / they | are working | are not working | Are they working? |

**Uses**: action in progress (*We **are reviewing** the contract.*), temporary situation, arranged plan (*I **am meeting** the client tomorrow.*).

**Clues**: *now, at the moment, currently, this week*.

## Spelling of -s

| Verb ending | Rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| consonant + **y** | y → **ies** | study → studies |
| **s, sh, ch, x, z, o** | + **es** | watch → watches, go → goes |
| the rest | + **s** | work → works |

## Traps for French speakers

- **"Je travaille ici depuis 2 ans"** ≠ present: *I have worked here for two years.*
- **State verbs** (*know, need, want, like, belong, own, understand*) are never continuous. *I **know** him* (not *I am knowing*).
- **-s only on the third person singular**: *The managers **work*** (plural, no -s), *The manager **works***.
