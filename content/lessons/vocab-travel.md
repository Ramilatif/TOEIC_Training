# Vocabulaire : les voyages

Billets, réservations, retards : le voyage d'affaires est un thème majeur des Parts 2, 3, 4 et 7.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **itinerary** | itinéraire | Her itinerary includes three cities. |
| **fare** | tarif | The fare has increased. |
| **boarding pass** | carte d'embarquement | Show your boarding pass at the gate. |
| **layover** | escale | We have a two-hour layover. |
| **round trip** | aller-retour | I booked a round-trip ticket. |
| **reservation** | réservation | I have a reservation under Lee. |
| **check in** | s'enregistrer | Check in two hours early. |
| **baggage claim** | retrait des bagages | Meet me at baggage claim. |
| **delay** | retard | There was a long delay. |
| **depart** | partir | The train departs at nine. |
| **accommodation** | hébergement | The company pays for accommodation. |
| **reimburse** | rembourser | We will reimburse your travel costs. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **book a flight** : réserver un vol
- **on business** : en voyage d'affaires
- **get stuck in traffic** : être coincé dans la circulation
- **pick up** : aller chercher
- **drop off** : déposer

## Astuce TOEIC

Pour les annonces d'aéroport (Part 4), notez la porte (gate), l'heure et la raison du retard : ce sont les trois questions classiques.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

<!-- lang:en -->
# Vocabulary: travel

Tickets, bookings, delays: business travel is a major theme in Parts 2, 3, 4 and 7.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **itinerary** | a detailed plan of a trip | Her itinerary includes three cities. |
| **fare** | the price of a ticket | The fare has increased. |
| **boarding pass** | a card allowing you to board | Show your boarding pass at the gate. |
| **layover** | a short stop between flights | We have a two-hour layover. |
| **round trip** | a trip there and back | I booked a round-trip ticket. |
| **reservation** | a booking | I have a reservation under Lee. |
| **check in** | to register on arrival | Check in two hours early. |
| **baggage claim** | area where bags are collected | Meet me at baggage claim. |
| **delay** | a late departure or arrival | There was a long delay. |
| **depart** | to leave | The train departs at nine. |
| **accommodation** | a place to stay | The company pays for accommodation. |
| **reimburse** | to pay back | We will reimburse your travel costs. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **book a flight**
- **on business**
- **get stuck in traffic**
- **pick up**
- **drop off**

## TOEIC tip

For airport announcements (Part 4), note the gate, the time and the reason for the delay: the three classic questions.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
