# La voix passive

On l'utilise quand l'**action** (ou celui qui la subit) compte plus que l'auteur.

## Formation

**Sujet + be (au bon temps) + participe passé** (+ *by* + auteur, facultatif).

| Temps | Actif | Passif |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Présent | They **send** the invoices. | The invoices **are sent**. |
| Prétérit | They **sent** the invoices. | The invoices **were sent**. |
| Present perfect | They **have sent** the invoices. | The invoices **have been sent**. |
| Futur | They **will send** the invoices. | The invoices **will be sent**. |
| Modal | They **must send** the invoices. | The invoices **must be sent**. |

## Comment la reconnaître au TOEIC

1. **Pas de complément d'objet après le blanc** : *The contract ___ signed* → passif (*was signed*).
2. **Le sujet est une chose** qui ne peut pas faire l'action : *The package ___ delivered* → *was delivered*.
3. **Verbes intransitifs** (happen, occur, arrive, appear, remain, last) ne se mettent **jamais** au passif.
4. **Après un modal** : modal + **be** + participe passé.

## Astuce

Si le verbe est suivi d'un complément (*She **sent** the report*), c'est l'actif. S'il est suivi de *by*, d'une préposition de lieu ou de rien, pensez au passif.

<!-- extra -->
## Pièges des francophones

- **Verbes intransitifs sans passif** : *happen, occur, arrive, rise, remain* ne se mettent jamais au passif. *The meeting **took place*** (pas *was taken place*).
- **to be + participe passé** : l'auxiliaire change de temps, le participe reste. *is made, was made, has been made, will be made, must be made*.
- **Agent « by »** : facultatif, utilisé seulement s'il est important. *The report was written **by** Ms. Kim.*
- **Pronom « on »** : le français « on dit que… » devient *It is said that…* ou *The product is said to…*.
- **Adjectif ou passif ?** *The store is closed* (état) / *The store is closed at 6 by the manager* (action).

## Exemples en contexte

- *All invoices **must be submitted** before the 5th.*
- *The new policy **has been approved** by the board.*

<!-- lang:en -->
# The passive voice

Use it when the **action** (or the receiver) matters more than who performs it.

## Formation

**Subject + be (right tense) + past participle** (+ *by* + agent, optional).

| Tense | Active | Passive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present | They **send** the invoices. | The invoices **are sent**. |
| Simple past | They **sent** the invoices. | The invoices **were sent**. |
| Present perfect | They **have sent** the invoices. | The invoices **have been sent**. |
| Future | They **will send** the invoices. | The invoices **will be sent**. |
| Modal | They **must send** the invoices. | The invoices **must be sent**. |

## Spotting it in the TOEIC

1. **No object after the blank**: *The contract ___ signed* → passive (*was signed*).
2. **A thing as subject** that cannot perform the action: *The package ___ delivered* → *was delivered*.
3. **Intransitive verbs** (happen, occur, arrive, appear, remain, last) are **never** passive.
4. **After a modal**: modal + **be** + past participle.

## Tip

If the verb is followed by an object (*She **sent** the report*), it is active. If it is followed by *by*, a place preposition or nothing, think passive.

## Traps for French speakers

- **Intransitive verbs have no passive**: *happen, occur, arrive, rise, remain* are never passive. *The meeting **took place*** (not "was taken place").
- **to be + past participle**: the auxiliary changes tense, the participle stays. *is made, was made, has been made, will be made, must be made*.
- **Agent "by"**: optional, used only if important. *The report was written **by** Ms. Kim.*
- **French "on"**: "on dit que…" becomes *It is said that…* or *The product is said to…*.
- **Adjective or passive?** *The store is closed* (state) / *The store is closed at 6 by the manager* (action).

## Examples in context

- *All invoices **must be submitted** before the 5th.*
- *The new policy **has been approved** by the board.*
