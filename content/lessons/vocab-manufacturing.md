- **in bulk** : en gros
- **on schedule** : dans les temps
- **production capacity** : capacité de production
- **safety regulations** : règles de sécurité
- **lead time** : délai de livraison

# Vocabulary: manufacturing and logistics

Factories, assembly lines, inventory and transport: a classic theme in Parts 3, 4 and 7.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **assembly line** | a series of workers or machines building a product | The assembly line runs 24 hours. |
| **inventory** | the goods a company holds | We check inventory weekly. |
| **warehouse** | a large building for storing goods | The warehouse is near the port. |
| **manufacture** | to make goods in large amounts | They manufacture car parts. |
| **quality control** | checking that products meet standards | Quality control found a defect. |
| **defect** | a fault in a product | The product has a defect. |
| **supplier** | a company that provides goods | Our supplier is reliable. |
| **raw materials** | basic substances used to make goods | Raw materials are costly. |
| **shipment** | goods sent together | The shipment left on Monday. |
| **freight** | goods transported by vehicle | Freight costs rose. |
| **output** | the amount produced | Output doubled this year. |
| **maintenance** | work to keep equipment running | Machine maintenance is scheduled. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **in bulk**
- **on schedule**
- **production capacity**
- **safety regulations**
- **lead time**
