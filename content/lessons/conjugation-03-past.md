# Le passé : prétérit et passé continu

## Prétérit (simple past)

| Type | Règle | Exemples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verbes réguliers | base + **-ed** | work → worked, arrive → arrived, stop → stopped, study → studied |
| Verbes irréguliers | forme à apprendre | go → **went**, buy → **bought**, send → **sent** |

**Négation et question** : *did* + base verbale, pour tous les sujets et tous les verbes sauf *be*.

- *She **did not attend** the meeting.* (pas *did not attended*)
- ***Did** they **sign** the contract?*
- *be* : *I/he/she/it **was** · we/you/they **were*** (*Was it ready? / It was not ready.*)

**Emploi** : action **terminée** à un moment précis du passé.

**Indices** : *yesterday, last week, in 2022, two days ago, when I was young*.

## Passé continu : was / were + -ing

| Personne | Forme |
| --- | --- |
| I / he / she / it | **was** working |
| we / you / they | **were** working |

**Emploi** : action **en cours** à un moment passé, ou action de fond interrompue par une autre.

*I **was reading** the report when the phone **rang**.*  (fond : *was reading* ; événement : *rang*)

## Pièges des francophones

- **Prétérit ≠ passé composé** : le français dit « j'ai fini hier », l'anglais impose *I **finished** yesterday*. Avec *yesterday, ago, last…*, **jamais** le present perfect.
- **Un seul marqueur de passé** : *Did you **go**?* (pas *Did you went?*).
- **Orthographe** : on double la consonne finale des mots courts (*stop → stopped*), y → ied (*carry → carried*), mais *play → played*.

# The past: simple past and past continuous

## Simple past

| Type | Rule | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular verbs | base + **-ed** | work → worked, arrive → arrived, stop → stopped, study → studied |
| Irregular verbs | forms to learn | go → **went**, buy → **bought**, send → **sent** |

**Negatives and questions**: *did* + base form, for every subject and every verb except *be*.

- *She **did not attend** the meeting.* (not *did not attended*)
- ***Did** they **sign** the contract?*
- *be*: *I/he/she/it **was** · we/you/they **were*** (*Was it ready? / It was not ready.*)

**Use**: **completed** action at a precise time in the past.

**Clues**: *yesterday, last week, in 2022, two days ago, when I was young*.

## Past continuous: was / were + -ing

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| I / he / she / it | **was** working |
| we / you / they | **were** working |

**Use**: action **in progress** at a past moment, or a background action interrupted by another.

*I **was reading** the report when the phone **rang**.*  (background: *was reading*; event: *rang*)

## Traps for French speakers

- **Simple past ≠ passé composé**: French says "j'ai fini hier", English requires *I **finished** yesterday*. With *yesterday, ago, last…*, **never** the present perfect.
- **One past marker only**: *Did you **go**?* (not *Did you went?*).
- **Spelling**: double the final consonant of short words (*stop → stopped*), y → ied (*carry → carried*), but *play → played*.
