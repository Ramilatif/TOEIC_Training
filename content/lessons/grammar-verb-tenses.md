# Les temps verbaux

Au TOEIC (Part 5 et 6), il faut choisir le bon temps grâce aux **indices de temps** de la phrase.

## Tableau récapitulatif

| Temps | Forme | Indices fréquents | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Présent simple | verbe (+ s) | every day, usually, always | The store **opens** at nine. |
| Présent continu | be + -ing | now, currently, at the moment | We **are reviewing** the contract now. |
| Prétérit | verbe + -ed / irrégulier | yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020 | She **joined** the firm last year. |
| Present perfect | have/has + participe passé | since, for, already, yet, recently | They **have signed** the deal. |
| Plus-que-parfait | had + participe passé | before, by the time, after (passé) | The train **had left** before we arrived. |
| Futur | will + verbe | tomorrow, next month, soon | The report **will be** ready on Friday. |
| Futur antérieur | will have + participe passé | by + date future | By June, we **will have hired** ten people. |

## Les pièges classiques

1. **Since / for** : *since* + point de départ (since 2019), *for* + durée (for five years) → toujours avec le present perfect.
2. **Après *when*, *as soon as*, *before*, *after*, *if* (futur)** : on met le **présent**, pas *will* : *As soon as the manager **arrives**, we will start.*
3. **Un repère passé précis** (yesterday, last week, in 2018) exclut le present perfect : on utilise le prétérit.
4. **Deux actions passées** : celle qui s'est passée en premier prend le plus-que-parfait.

## Astuce TOEIC

Repérez d'abord le mot-indice (since, yesterday, by the time, next week…), éliminez les temps incompatibles, puis vérifiez l'accord avec le sujet.

# Verb tenses

In TOEIC Parts 5 and 6, you pick the right tense thanks to the **time clues** in the sentence.

## Summary table

| Tense | Form | Common clues | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Simple present | verb (+ s) | every day, usually, always | The store **opens** at nine. |
| Present continuous | be + -ing | now, currently, at the moment | We **are reviewing** the contract now. |
| Simple past | verb + -ed / irregular | yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020 | She **joined** the firm last year. |
| Present perfect | have/has + past participle | since, for, already, yet, recently | They **have signed** the deal. |
| Past perfect | had + past participle | before, by the time, after (past) | The train **had left** before we arrived. |
| Future | will + verb | tomorrow, next month, soon | The report **will be** ready on Friday. |
| Future perfect | will have + past participle | by + future date | By June, we **will have hired** ten people. |

## Classic traps

1. **Since / for**: *since* + starting point (since 2019), *for* + duration (for five years), both with the present perfect.
2. **After *when*, *as soon as*, *before*, *after*, *if* (future meaning)** use the **present**, not *will*: *As soon as the manager **arrives**, we will start.*
3. **A specific past time** (yesterday, last week, in 2018) rules out the present perfect: use the simple past.
4. **Two past actions**: the one that happened first takes the past perfect.

## TOEIC tip

Spot the clue word first (since, yesterday, by the time, next week…), eliminate incompatible tenses, then check agreement with the subject.
