# Vocabulaire : collocations et expressions

Les collocations (verbe + nom, adjectif + nom) sont très testées en Part 5. Apprenez-les en bloc plutôt que mot à mot.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **make a decision** | prendre une décision | We made a decision today. |
| **meet a deadline** | respecter un délai | Meet the deadline. |
| **reach an agreement** | parvenir à un accord | They reached an agreement. |
| **raise a concern** | soulever une préoccupation | She raised a concern. |
| **take responsibility** | assumer la responsabilité | He took responsibility. |
| **conduct a survey** | mener une enquête | We conduct surveys. |
| **run a business** | diriger une entreprise | She runs a business. |
| **submit a report** | remettre un rapport | Submit your report. |
| **pay attention to** | faire attention à | Pay attention to details. |
| **keep in touch** | rester en contact | Let's keep in touch. |
| **lose track of** | perdre le fil de | I lost track of time. |
| **be subject to** | être soumis à | Prices are subject to change. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **heavy traffic** : circulation dense
- **tight schedule** : emploi du temps serré
- **strong demand** : forte demande
- **severe weather** : mauvais temps
- **a wide range of** : une large gamme de

## Astuce TOEIC

En Part 5, si quatre verbes ont le même sens, la collocation décide : cherchez celui qui « sonne » naturellement avec le nom.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Vocabulary: collocations and expressions

Collocations (verb + noun, adjective + noun) are heavily tested in Part 5. Learn them as chunks, not word by word.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **make a decision** | to decide | We made a decision today. |
| **meet a deadline** | to finish on time | Meet the deadline. |
| **reach an agreement** | to settle | They reached an agreement. |
| **raise a concern** | to mention a worry | She raised a concern. |
| **take responsibility** | to accept blame or duty | He took responsibility. |
| **conduct a survey** | to carry out a survey | We conduct surveys. |
| **run a business** | to manage a company | She runs a business. |
| **submit a report** | to hand in a report | Submit your report. |
| **pay attention to** | to focus on | Pay attention to details. |
| **keep in touch** | to stay in contact | Let's keep in touch. |
| **lose track of** | to no longer know where something is | I lost track of time. |
| **be subject to** | to be affected by | Prices are subject to change. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **heavy traffic**
- **tight schedule**
- **strong demand**
- **severe weather**
- **a wide range of**

## TOEIC tip

In Part 5, if four verbs mean roughly the same, the collocation decides: pick the one that sounds natural with the noun.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
