# Vocabulaire : ressources humaines

Recrutement, évaluation, avantages : les RH apparaissent en Parts 3, 4, 6 et 7.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **recruit** | recruter | We plan to recruit ten engineers. |
| **applicant** | candidat | Each applicant sent a résumé. |
| **résumé** | CV | Attach your résumé. |
| **interview** | entretien | The interview lasts an hour. |
| **promotion** | promotion | She earned a promotion. |
| **benefits** | avantages sociaux | Full-time staff receive benefits. |
| **payroll** | paie | Payroll is processed monthly. |
| **retire** | prendre sa retraite | He will retire in June. |
| **resign** | démissionner | She resigned last week. |
| **probation** | période d'essai | A six-month probation applies. |
| **training** | formation | Training starts Monday. |
| **performance review** | évaluation annuelle | Your performance review is in May. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **fill a vacancy** : pourvoir un poste
- **job opening** : poste à pourvoir
- **hand in one's notice** : donner sa démission
- **be eligible for** : avoir droit à
- **on leave** : en congé

## Astuce TOEIC

« Applicant » (celui qui postule) et « candidate » (candidat retenu pour l'étape suivante) sont proches : le TOEIC emploie surtout « applicant ».

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Vocabulary: human resources

Recruitment, reviews, benefits: HR topics appear in Parts 3, 4, 6 and 7.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **recruit** | to find and hire new staff | We plan to recruit ten engineers. |
| **applicant** | a person who applies for a job | Each applicant sent a résumé. |
| **résumé** | a document summarizing a career | Attach your résumé. |
| **interview** | a formal job meeting | The interview lasts an hour. |
| **promotion** | a move to a higher position | She earned a promotion. |
| **benefits** | extras such as insurance | Full-time staff receive benefits. |
| **payroll** | the list of paid employees | Payroll is processed monthly. |
| **retire** | to stop working at old age | He will retire in June. |
| **resign** | to leave a job by choice | She resigned last week. |
| **probation** | a trial period at work | A six-month probation applies. |
| **training** | teaching of job skills | Training starts Monday. |
| **performance review** | a formal assessment of work | Your performance review is in May. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **fill a vacancy**
- **job opening**
- **hand in one's notice**
- **be eligible for**
- **on leave**

## TOEIC tip

'Applicant' (one who applies) and 'candidate' are close: the TOEIC mostly uses 'applicant'.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
