# Le passif à tous les temps

**Principe** : on conjugue **be** au temps voulu, puis on ajoute le **participe passé**.

| Temps | Actif | Passif |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Présent simple | They **send** the invoice. | The invoice **is sent**. |
| Présent continu | They **are sending** it. | It **is being sent**. |
| Prétérit | They **sent** it. | It **was sent**. |
| Passé continu | They **were sending** it. | It **was being sent**. |
| Present perfect | They **have sent** it. | It **has been sent**. |
| Plus-que-parfait | They **had sent** it. | It **had been sent**. |
| Futur (will) | They **will send** it. | It **will be sent**. |
| Futur antérieur | They **will have sent** it. | It **will have been sent**. |
| Modal | They **must send** it. | It **must be sent**. |
| Infinitif | They want **to send** it. | It needs **to be sent**. |

## Astuces

- **L'auxiliaire porte le temps**, le participe ne change jamais : *is / was / has been / will be* **+ sent**.
- **Agent** : *by* + qui fait l'action, uniquement s'il est utile.
- **Verbes sans passif** : *happen, occur, arrive, appear, seem, remain* (verbes intransitifs).

## Pièges des francophones

- **« On » français** : *We are told*, *It is said that* (le sujet est inconnu).
- **Participe passé irrégulier obligatoire** : *The report was **written*** (pas *wrote*).
- **Passif progressif** : *The building **is being renovated*** (are/is being + participe).
- **After a modal** : *The form **must be completed*** (be + participe, jamais *must completed*).

# The passive in every tense

**Principle**: conjugate **be** in the required tense, then add the **past participle**.

| Tense | Active | Passive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple present | They **send** the invoice. | The invoice **is sent**. |
| Present continuous | They **are sending** it. | It **is being sent**. |
| Simple past | They **sent** it. | It **was sent**. |
| Past continuous | They **were sending** it. | It **was being sent**. |
| Present perfect | They **have sent** it. | It **has been sent**. |
| Past perfect | They **had sent** it. | It **had been sent**. |
| Future (will) | They **will send** it. | It **will be sent**. |
| Future perfect | They **will have sent** it. | It **will have been sent**. |
| Modal | They **must send** it. | It **must be sent**. |
| Infinitive | They want **to send** it. | It needs **to be sent**. |

## Tips

- **The auxiliary carries the tense**; the participle never changes: *is / was / has been / will be* **+ sent**.
- **Agent**: *by* + doer, only if useful.
- **Verbs with no passive**: *happen, occur, arrive, appear, seem, remain* (intransitive verbs).

## Traps for French speakers

- **French "on"**: *We are told*, *It is said that* (unknown subject).
- **Irregular participle required**: *The report was **written*** (not *wrote*).
- **Progressive passive**: *The building **is being renovated*** (are/is being + participle).
- **After a modal**: *The form **must be completed*** (be + participle, never *must completed*).
