# Plus-que-parfait et formes du futur

## Plus-que-parfait (past perfect) : had + participe passé

*Le plus-que-parfait place une action **avant** une autre action passée.*

*When we arrived, the meeting **had** already **started**.* (d'abord *started*, ensuite *arrived*)

| Forme | Exemple |
| --- | --- |
| Affirmatif | *She **had left**.* |
| Négatif | *They **had not (hadn't)** signed.* |
| Question | ***Had** you finished?* |

**Indices** : *by the time, before, after, already, just, when*.

## Les formes du futur

| Forme | Construction | Emploi | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **will** | will + base | décision immédiate, promesse, prévision | *I **will** send it today.* |
| **going to** | am/is/are going to + base | intention, signe visible | *We **are going to** hire two people.* |
| **présent continu** | am/is/are + -ing | rendez-vous arrangé | *I **am meeting** the client at 3.* |
| **présent simple** | présent | horaire officiel | *The train **leaves** at 6.* |
| **futur continu** | will be + -ing | action en cours à un moment futur | *At noon I **will be presenting**.* |
| **futur antérieur** | will have + participe passé | action terminée **avant** un moment futur | *By Friday, we **will have finished**.* |

## Subordonnée de temps : jamais de will

Après *when, as soon as, before, after, until, by the time, once, if* : **présent**.

*We will start **when** the manager **arrives**.* (pas *will arrive*)

## Pièges des francophones

- **Concordance** : *By the time the auditors arrive, we **will have updated** the files* (présent dans la subordonnée, futur antérieur dans la principale).
- **Plus-que-parfait avec *before/by the time* au passé** : *He had left **before** I **arrived**.*
- **Pas de *will* après *if*** : *If it rains, we **will** cancel* (pas *if it will rain*).
- **Soyez attentif à l'ordre des événements** : l'action qui s'est produite **en premier** prend *had*.

# Past perfect and future forms

## Past perfect: had + past participle

*The past perfect places one action **before** another past action.*

*When we arrived, the meeting **had** already **started**.* (first *started*, then *arrived*)

| Form | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Affirmative | *She **had left**.* |
| Negative | *They **had not (hadn't)** signed.* |
| Question | ***Had** you finished?* |

**Clues**: *by the time, before, after, already, just, when*.

## The future forms

| Form | Construction | Use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **will** | will + base | instant decision, promise, prediction | *I **will** send it today.* |
| **going to** | am/is/are going to + base | intention, visible sign | *We **are going to** hire two people.* |
| **present continuous** | am/is/are + -ing | arranged meeting | *I **am meeting** the client at 3.* |
| **simple present** | present | official timetable | *The train **leaves** at 6.* |
| **future continuous** | will be + -ing | action in progress at a future moment | *At noon I **will be presenting**.* |
| **future perfect** | will have + past participle | action finished **before** a future moment | *By Friday, we **will have finished**.* |

## Time clauses: never will

After *when, as soon as, before, after, until, by the time, once, if*: **present**.

*We will start **when** the manager **arrives**.* (not *will arrive*)

## Traps for French speakers

- **Sequence of tenses**: *By the time the auditors arrive, we **will have updated** the files* (present in the subordinate clause, future perfect in the main clause).
- **Past perfect with past *before/by the time*** : *He had left **before** I **arrived**.*
- **No *will* after *if***: *If it rains, we **will** cancel* (not *if it will rain*).
- **Mind the order of events**: the action that happened **first** takes *had*.
