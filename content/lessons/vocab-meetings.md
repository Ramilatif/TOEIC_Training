# Vocabulaire : les réunions

Organisation, ordre du jour, décisions : le vocabulaire des réunions est omniprésent en Parts 3, 4 et 7.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **agenda** | ordre du jour | The agenda has four items. |
| **minutes** | compte rendu | Who will take the minutes? |
| **attendee** | participant | Each attendee received a folder. |
| **postpone** | reporter | The meeting was postponed. |
| **reschedule** | reprogrammer | Can we reschedule for Friday? |
| **conference call** | conférence téléphonique | The conference call starts at noon. |
| **presentation** | présentation | Her presentation was clear. |
| **brainstorm** | faire un brainstorming | Let's brainstorm solutions. |
| **adjourn** | lever la séance | The chair adjourned the meeting. |
| **quorum** | quorum | We have a quorum, so we can vote. |
| **agree on** | s'accorder sur | We agreed on a date. |
| **follow up** | assurer le suivi | I will follow up by email. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **hold a meeting** : tenir une réunion
- **chair a meeting** : présider une réunion
- **set up a meeting** : organiser une réunion
- **call off** : annuler
- **wrap up** : conclure

## Astuce TOEIC

En Part 3, « call off » et « put off » reformulent souvent « cancel » et « postpone ». Repérez les paraphrases.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

<!-- extra -->
## Mots complémentaires

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **chair** | présider | Ms. Kim will chair the meeting. |
| **motion** | proposition (soumise au vote) | The motion passed. |
| **consensus** | consensus | We reached a consensus. |
| **minutes** | compte rendu | Please approve the minutes. |
| **action item** | tâche à réaliser | Add it as an action item. |
| **wrap up** | conclure | Let's wrap up. |

## À retenir

Au TOEIC, *minutes* = compte rendu (pas « minutes » de durée). Les mots *agenda*, *adjourn*, *follow up* signalent presque toujours une réunion.

# Vocabulary: meetings

Scheduling, agendas, decisions: meeting vocabulary is everywhere in Parts 3, 4 and 7.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **agenda** | a list of topics for a meeting | The agenda has four items. |
| **minutes** | the written record of a meeting | Who will take the minutes? |
| **attendee** | a person who attends | Each attendee received a folder. |
| **postpone** | to move to a later time | The meeting was postponed. |
| **reschedule** | to change the time of an event | Can we reschedule for Friday? |
| **conference call** | a phone meeting with several people | The conference call starts at noon. |
| **presentation** | a talk with slides | Her presentation was clear. |
| **brainstorm** | to produce ideas as a group | Let's brainstorm solutions. |
| **adjourn** | to end a meeting | The chair adjourned the meeting. |
| **quorum** | the minimum number of people required | We have a quorum, so we can vote. |
| **agree on** | to reach the same decision | We agreed on a date. |
| **follow up** | to take further action afterwards | I will follow up by email. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **hold a meeting**
- **chair a meeting**
- **set up a meeting**
- **call off**
- **wrap up**

## TOEIC tip

In Part 3, 'call off' and 'put off' often reword 'cancel' and 'postpone'. Watch for paraphrases.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
