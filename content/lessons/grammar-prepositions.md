# Les prépositions

Les prépositions du TOEIC sont de trois types : **temps/lieu**, **expressions fixes** (verbe/adjectif + préposition) et **prépositions de cause/concession**.

## Temps et lieu

| Préposition | Emploi | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **at** | heure précise, point | at 9 a.m., at the entrance |
| **on** | jour, date, surface | on Monday, on May 3, on the desk |
| **in** | mois, année, saison, durée à venir, espace fermé | in June, in 2025, in two weeks, in the office |
| **by** | avant / au plus tard | by Friday |
| **for** | durée | for three years |
| **since** | point de départ | since March |
| **during** | pendant un événement | during the conference |
| **within** | dans un délai | within five days |

## Prépositions de sens proche

- **between** (deux) / **among** (plus de deux)
- **through** (à travers) / **throughout** (partout, pendant tout)
- **despite / in spite of** (concession) + nom ≠ **because of / due to** (cause) + nom

## Expressions fixes à retenir

*responsible **for**, interested **in**, depend **on**, comply **with**, in charge **of**, similar **to**, different **from**, capable **of**, result **in**, apply **for**.*

## Astuce

Après une préposition, on trouve un **nom** ou un **gérondif**, jamais un verbe conjugué. *Despite **the delay***, *Despite **being** late*.

<!-- extra -->
## Pièges des francophones

- **in time / on time** : *in time* = assez tôt pour quelque chose ; *on time* = à l'heure prévue. *The train arrived on time.*
- **during / for** : *during* + événement (*during the meeting*), *for* + durée (*for two hours*).
- **until / by** : *until* = jusqu'à (action qui dure), *by* = au plus tard (échéance). *Stay until 5* / *Finish by 5*.
- **Pas de « depend of »** : on dit *depend **on***. Pas de « discuss about » : *discuss* se construit sans préposition.
- **Après une préposition** : nom ou *-ing*. *Thank you for **coming**.*

## Exemples en contexte

- *Applications must be submitted **by** March 15.*
- *The office will be closed **during** the renovation.*
- *Our prices are lower **than** those of our competitors.*

# Prepositions

TOEIC prepositions come in three kinds: **time/place**, **fixed expressions** (verb/adjective + preposition) and **cause/contrast prepositions**.

## Time and place

| Preposition | Use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **at** | exact time, point | at 9 a.m., at the entrance |
| **on** | day, date, surface | on Monday, on May 3, on the desk |
| **in** | month, year, season, future period, enclosed space | in June, in 2025, in two weeks, in the office |
| **by** | before / no later than | by Friday |
| **for** | duration | for three years |
| **since** | starting point | since March |
| **during** | throughout an event | during the conference |
| **within** | inside a time limit | within five days |

## Close meanings

- **between** (two) / **among** (more than two)
- **through** (across) / **throughout** (everywhere, the whole time)
- **despite / in spite of** (contrast) + noun ≠ **because of / due to** (cause) + noun

## Fixed expressions to learn

*responsible **for**, interested **in**, depend **on**, comply **with**, in charge **of**, similar **to**, different **from**, capable **of**, result **in**, apply **for**.*

## Tip

After a preposition comes a **noun** or a **gerund**, never a conjugated verb. *Despite **the delay***, *Despite **being** late*.

## Traps for French speakers

- **in time / on time**: *in time* = early enough for something; *on time* = at the scheduled time. *The train arrived on time.*
- **during / for**: *during* + event (*during the meeting*), *for* + length (*for two hours*).
- **until / by**: *until* = up to (ongoing), *by* = no later than (deadline). *Stay until 5* / *Finish by 5*.
- **No "depend of"**: say *depend **on***. No "discuss about": *discuss* takes no preposition.
- **After a preposition**: noun or *-ing*. *Thank you for **coming**.*

## Examples in context

- *Applications must be submitted **by** March 15.*
- *The office will be closed **during** the renovation.*
- *Our prices are lower **than** those of our competitors.*
