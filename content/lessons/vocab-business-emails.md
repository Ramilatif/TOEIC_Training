# Courriels et courriers professionnels

Les e-mails, lettres et mémos sont très fréquents en Part 7. Connaître leurs formules figées permet de lire plus vite et de repérer l'objet du message.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I am writing to** | je vous écris pour | I am writing to inquire about your services. |
| **attached** | ci-joint | Please find the report attached. |
| **enclosed** | ci-inclus (courrier papier) | A brochure is enclosed. |
| **regarding** | concernant | Regarding your order, it has shipped. |
| **at your earliest convenience** | dès que possible | Reply at your earliest convenience. |
| **please be advised** | veuillez noter que | Please be advised that the office is closed. |
| **inquire** | se renseigner | I would like to inquire about prices. |
| **confirm** | confirmer | Please confirm your attendance. |
| **apologize for** | s'excuser de | We apologize for the inconvenience. |
| **sincerely** | cordialement | Sincerely, Jane Doe |
| **forward** | transférer | I forwarded the message to Ms. Lim. |
| **reply** | répondre | Please reply by Friday. |
| **schedule** | planifier, planning | Let us schedule a call. |
| **proposal** | proposition | We submitted a proposal. |
| **deadline** | date limite | The deadline is Monday. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **Dear Sir or Madam** : Madame, Monsieur
- **Thank you for your prompt reply** : merci pour votre réponse rapide
- **Please do not hesitate to contact me** : n'hésitez pas à me contacter
- **I look forward to hearing from you** : j'attends votre réponse
- **Best regards** : bien cordialement
- **in response to your inquiry** : en réponse à votre demande

## Astuce TOEIC

Le premier paragraphe donne presque toujours l'objet. En Part 7, lisez l'objet (*Subject*), la première et la dernière phrase avant de répondre aux questions.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Business emails and letters

Emails, letters and memos are very frequent in Part 7. Knowing their set phrases lets you read faster and spot the purpose of the message.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I am writing to** | state the reason for writing | I am writing to inquire about your services. |
| **attached** | included with the message | Please find the report attached. |
| **enclosed** | inside the envelope | A brochure is enclosed. |
| **regarding** | about | Regarding your order, it has shipped. |
| **at your earliest convenience** | as soon as you can | Reply at your earliest convenience. |
| **please be advised** | formal notice | Please be advised that the office is closed. |
| **inquire** | ask for information | I would like to inquire about prices. |
| **confirm** | state that something is certain | Please confirm your attendance. |
| **apologize for** | say sorry | We apologize for the inconvenience. |
| **sincerely** | formal closing | Sincerely, Jane Doe |
| **forward** | send on | I forwarded the message to Ms. Lim. |
| **reply** | answer | Please reply by Friday. |
| **schedule** | plan a time | Let us schedule a call. |
| **proposal** | formal offer | We submitted a proposal. |
| **deadline** | final date | The deadline is Monday. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **Dear Sir or Madam**
- **Thank you for your prompt reply**
- **Please do not hesitate to contact me**
- **I look forward to hearing from you**
- **Best regards**
- **in response to your inquiry**

## TOEIC tip

The first paragraph almost always states the purpose. In Part 7, read the subject line, the first and the last sentence before answering the questions.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
