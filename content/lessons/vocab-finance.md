# Vocabulaire : finance et banque

Budgets, factures, intérêts : la finance est un thème récurrent des Parts 5, 6 et 7.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **budget** | budget | The budget was approved. |
| **invoice** | facture | The invoice is due in 30 days. |
| **revenue** | chiffre d'affaires | Revenue rose by 5 percent. |
| **expense** | dépense | Travel expenses are reimbursed. |
| **profit** | bénéfice | The firm made a profit. |
| **deposit** | dépôt / acompte | A deposit is required. |
| **withdraw** | retirer | You can withdraw cash here. |
| **interest rate** | taux d'intérêt | The interest rate is 4 percent. |
| **tax** | impôt / taxe | Sales tax is included. |
| **balance** | solde | Check your account balance. |
| **overdue** | en retard de paiement | Your invoice is overdue. |
| **audit** | audit | An audit is planned for June. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **pay in full** : payer intégralement
- **make a payment** : effectuer un paiement
- **cut costs** : réduire les coûts
- **fiscal year** : exercice fiscal
- **due date** : date d'échéance

## Astuce TOEIC

Ne confondez pas « revenue » (recettes) et « profit » (bénéfice) : une entreprise peut avoir un chiffre d'affaires élevé et un bénéfice faible.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Vocabulary: finance and banking

Budgets, invoices, interest: finance is a recurring theme in Parts 5, 6 and 7.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **budget** | a plan for spending money | The budget was approved. |
| **invoice** | a bill listing goods and prices | The invoice is due in 30 days. |
| **revenue** | income from sales | Revenue rose by 5 percent. |
| **expense** | money spent | Travel expenses are reimbursed. |
| **profit** | money left after costs | The firm made a profit. |
| **deposit** | money put in a bank or paid in advance | A deposit is required. |
| **withdraw** | to take money out | You can withdraw cash here. |
| **interest rate** | percentage charged on a loan | The interest rate is 4 percent. |
| **tax** | money paid to the government | Sales tax is included. |
| **balance** | the amount in an account | Check your account balance. |
| **overdue** | past the payment date | Your invoice is overdue. |
| **audit** | an official examination of accounts | An audit is planned for June. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **pay in full**
- **make a payment**
- **cut costs**
- **fiscal year**
- **due date**

## TOEIC tip

Do not confuse 'revenue' (income) with 'profit' (what is left): a firm can have high revenue and low profit.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
