# Les auxiliaires be, have et do

Ces trois verbes servent à **conjuguer tous les autres**. Les maîtriser règle la moitié des questions de conjugaison du TOEIC.

## Les formes

| Verbe | Présent | Prétérit | Participe passé | Forme en -ing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **be** | I **am** · he/she/it **is** · we/you/they **are** | I/he/she/it **was** · we/you/they **were** | **been** | **being** |
| **have** | I/you/we/they **have** · he/she/it **has** | **had** | **had** | **having** |
| **do** | I/you/we/they **do** · he/she/it **does** | **did** | **done** | **doing** |

## À quoi servent-ils ?

| Auxiliaire | Rôle | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **be** + -ing | forme progressive | *She **is** writing a report.* |
| **be** + participe passé | passif | *The invoice **was** sent.* |
| **have** + participe passé | temps parfaits | *We **have** finished.* |
| **do** | questions et négations au présent / prétérit | ***Does** he work here? He **did not** come.* |

## Négation et question

- **be** et les modaux n'ont pas besoin de *do* : *Is she here?* / *She is not here.*
- Les autres verbes utilisent *do/does/did* + **base verbale** : *Do you **need** help?* / *He does not **know**.*
- Après *do/does/did*, le verbe reste à la **forme de base** : *Did she **call**?* (pas *called*).

## Pièges des francophones

- **« J'ai 25 ans » = *I am 25*** (be), jamais *I have 25 years*.
- **Pas d'auxiliaire devant un verbe ordinaire** : *I am agree* est faux → *I agree*.
- **has / have** : *has* uniquement avec he, she, it (ou un sujet singulier). *The team **has** a deadline.*
- ***do* + -s impossible** : *Does he works?* est faux → *Does he **work**?*

# The auxiliaries be, have and do

These three verbs are used to **build every other verb form**. Mastering them solves half of the conjugation questions on the TOEIC.

## The forms

| Verb | Present | Past | Past participle | -ing form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **be** | I **am** · he/she/it **is** · we/you/they **are** | I/he/she/it **was** · we/you/they **were** | **been** | **being** |
| **have** | I/you/we/they **have** · he/she/it **has** | **had** | **had** | **having** |
| **do** | I/you/we/they **do** · he/she/it **does** | **did** | **done** | **doing** |

## What are they used for?

| Auxiliary | Role | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **be** + -ing | progressive form | *She **is** writing a report.* |
| **be** + past participle | passive | *The invoice **was** sent.* |
| **have** + past participle | perfect tenses | *We **have** finished.* |
| **do** | questions and negatives in the present / past | ***Does** he work here? He **did not** come.* |

## Negatives and questions

- **be** and modals do not need *do*: *Is she here?* / *She is not here.*
- Other verbs use *do/does/did* + **base form**: *Do you **need** help?* / *He does not **know**.*
- After *do/does/did* the verb stays in the **base form**: *Did she **call**?* (not *called*).

## Traps for French speakers

- **French "j'ai 25 ans" = *I am 25*** (be), never *I have 25 years*.
- **No auxiliary before an ordinary verb**: "I am agree" is wrong → *I agree*.
- **has / have**: *has* only with he, she, it (or a singular subject). *The team **has** a deadline.*
- **No -s after *does***: "Does he works?" is wrong → *Does he **work**?*
