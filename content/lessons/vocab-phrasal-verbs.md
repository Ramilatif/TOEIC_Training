# Verbes à particule (phrasal verbs)

Les verbes à particule (verbe + préposition ou adverbe) sont omniprésents au TOEIC, surtout en Part 5 et dans les dialogues Listening.

## Mots essentiels

| Mot | Traduction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **carry out** | mener à bien, exécuter | They carried out a survey. |
| **set up** | installer, organiser | We set up a meeting. |
| **look into** | examiner, enquêter sur | We will look into the matter. |
| **fill out** | remplir (formulaire) | Please fill out this form. |
| **turn down** | refuser | She turned down the offer. |
| **call off** | annuler | The event was called off. |
| **put off** | reporter | They put off the launch. |
| **run out of** | manquer de | We ran out of paper. |
| **take over** | reprendre, racheter | A larger firm took over the company. |
| **follow up** | assurer le suivi | I will follow up by email. |
| **point out** | souligner, signaler | He pointed out an error. |
| **sign up for** | s'inscrire à | Sign up for the workshop. |
| **figure out** | comprendre, trouver | We figured out the problem. |
| **hand in** | remettre | Hand in your report. |
| **catch up on** | rattraper | I need to catch up on emails. |
| **get back to** | recontacter | I will get back to you. |

## Collocations et expressions utiles

- **bring up a topic** : aborder un sujet
- **come up with an idea** : trouver une idée
- **go over the figures** : passer en revue les chiffres
- **deal with a complaint** : traiter une réclamation
- **pick up a package** : récupérer un colis
- **drop off a form** : déposer un formulaire

## Astuce TOEIC

Au TOEIC, un verbe à particule est souvent remplacé dans la bonne réponse par un synonyme formel (*postpone* pour *put off*). Apprenez les deux versions.

Ajoutez ces mots à vos flashcards pour les réviser avec la répétition espacée.

# Phrasal verbs

Phrasal verbs (verb + preposition or adverb) are everywhere on the TOEIC, especially in Part 5 and in Listening dialogues.

## Key words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **carry out** | perform | They carried out a survey. |
| **set up** | establish | We set up a meeting. |
| **look into** | investigate | We will look into the matter. |
| **fill out** | complete a form | Please fill out this form. |
| **turn down** | reject | She turned down the offer. |
| **call off** | cancel | The event was called off. |
| **put off** | postpone | They put off the launch. |
| **run out of** | use up | We ran out of paper. |
| **take over** | assume control | A larger firm took over the company. |
| **follow up** | continue contact | I will follow up by email. |
| **point out** | indicate | He pointed out an error. |
| **sign up for** | register | Sign up for the workshop. |
| **figure out** | solve | We figured out the problem. |
| **hand in** | submit | Hand in your report. |
| **catch up on** | update oneself | I need to catch up on emails. |
| **get back to** | reply to | I will get back to you. |

## Useful collocations and expressions

- **bring up a topic**
- **come up with an idea**
- **go over the figures**
- **deal with a complaint**
- **pick up a package**
- **drop off a form**

## TOEIC tip

On the TOEIC, a phrasal verb is often replaced in the right answer by a formal synonym (*postpone* for *put off*). Learn both.

Add these words to your flashcards to review them with spaced repetition.
